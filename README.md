Set Up Your MP3 Player!:
*preferably use the Launcher Cardputer app.

 1-install or flash the .BIN onto your Cardputer ADV.
 2-create a folder in the in the root of your SD card named "cardplayer".
 3-use the Spotidown website to download your favorite Spotify songs.
 4-put the songs into your "cardplayer" folder.
 5-optional: put the themes.csv and keys.cfg files in the same folder for more customization.
 6-listen to music with cool visuals anywhere you go!
